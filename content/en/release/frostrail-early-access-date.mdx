## What the Launch Build Will Actually Contain

This is where Frostrail separates itself from the usual Early Access survival drop. The store page describes the Early Access version as fully playable with core mechanics in place, while acknowledging that some non-blocking bugs may appear as early features are integrated.

The core loop, as described by the developers:

- **The Eden Engine:** fuel and power management aboard an armored, steam-powered train that serves as both refuge and transport.
- **Train progression:** reinforced walls, crafted weapon stations, engine upgrades, and expanded carriages with crafting stations and storage.
- **Expeditions:** scavenging ruined settlements and wreckage, plus descending into bunkers and strongholds where the best salvage sits behind the worst danger.
- **Heat as a resource:** blizzards blind, slow, and suffocate; warmth must be rationed, and every step away from the train is a gamble.
- **Combat:** revenants, eldritch horrors, wolves, and humanoid enemies, fought solo or in co-op with crafted weapons and equipment.

The narrative frame is a fallen empire twisted by the Emperor's pact with the Void, with the player cast as one of the Penitent Gardeners — mercy-killers and monster hunters working through frozen desolation toward some kind of salvation.

## How to Be Ready on Day One

The Frostrail early access date will not be preceded by months of marketing noise — indie Early Access launches tend to arrive with a trailer, a patch note, and a price tag. Being ready is mostly about setup:

- **Wishlist and enable notifications** so a launch-day price change does not catch you off guard.
- **Free up 25 GB** on an SSD; train physics and blizzard effects benefit noticeably from fast storage.
- **Line up your co-op group in advance.** Survival games live or die on whether your friends actually show up on launch weekend.
- **Agree on roles before you play.** Driver, scavenger, and defender is the natural split, and it prevents the classic "everyone left the train" disaster.
- **Expect bugs and report them.** The developers have explicitly asked for bug reports and suggestions through Discord and Steam forums, and Early Access builds always ship with rough edges.
- **Do not over-invest in a single save.** Balance changes and content additions over the first year can and will reshape the progression curve.
