## What Frostrail Actually Plays Like

Release timing is only half the story. The setting is a frozen empire ruined by the Emperor's pact with the Void, where revenants and eldritch horrors roam ice-entombed ruins. You play as one of the Penitent Gardeners, an order that travels aboard an armored, steam-powered train, purging the cursed and granting mercy to the lost.

The train is the whole game's spine. You fuel the Eden Engine, manage its power, and operate it to push deeper into the wasteland. As the run gets harder, the train has to grow with you: reinforced walls, weapon stations, an upgraded engine, new carriages, crafting stations, and storage. It becomes a moving fortress.

Away from the train, the loop is scavenging under pressure. Every step into the cold is a gamble — heat is survival, blizzards blind and slow you, and bunkers hide both salvage and death. Combat is solo or co-op, and the mature content description is blunt about it: wolves, humanoids, and blood.
