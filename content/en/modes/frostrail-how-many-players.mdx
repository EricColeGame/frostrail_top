## Tips for Assembling a Frostrail Crew

Whether the cap lands at two, four, or somewhere in between, a little prep goes a long way:

- **Decide the host in advance.** Host-side saves are common in co-op survival games, and nothing kills momentum like losing a run because someone logged off.
- **Agree on a session length.** Drop-in/drop-out is forgiving, but a train-based game rewards long, uninterrupted pushes.
- **Assign one person to inventory.** Shared stashes turn into chaos without a designated organizer.
- **Plan your first run as a learning run.** Scavenging routes and combat rhythm matter more than early upgrades.
- **Set voice chat expectations.** Survival shooters live and die on callouts, especially when someone is driving and can't see the flanks.
- **Keep a solo save on the side.** If you want to see the story at your own pace, don't lock yourself into group scheduling.
