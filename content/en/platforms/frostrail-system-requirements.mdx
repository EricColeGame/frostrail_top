## Why the Frostrail System Requirements Matter Before You Board

The Frostrail system requirements are the first thing any PC player should read before wishlisting this frostbitten co-op survival game, because the listed hardware floor is higher than the snowy art style suggests. Frostrail puts you aboard an armored, steam-powered train — the Eden Engine — and asks you to keep it running while you scavenge a wasteland full of revenants and eldritch horrors. That means a lot of moving parts: dynamic weather, blizzards that blind and slow you, crafting stations, base-building on rails, and co-op sessions that sync all of it.

If your rig lands below the minimum, you will not be purging the cursed anytime soon. Below is a complete breakdown of the published Frostrail system requirements, what each component actually does for you, and how to tell whether your build clears the bar before launch day.

## Frostrail System Requirements at a Glance

The official listing on the [Frostrail Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3517740/Frostrail/) splits the specs into two tiers. Both require a 64-bit processor and a 64-bit operating system, which rules out any 32-bit Windows install.

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OS | Windows 10 (64-bit) | Windows 10 (64-bit) |
| Processor | Intel Core i7-6700K or AMD Ryzen 5 5500 | Intel Core i5-11400T or AMD Ryzen 5 |
| Memory | 16 GB RAM | 32 GB RAM |
| Graphics | GeForce RTX 3060 or Radeon RX 5600 XT | GeForce RTX 4060 or Radeon RX 7600 |
| Storage | 25 GB available space | 25 GB available space |
| Architecture | 64-bit CPU and OS | 64-bit CPU and OS |

Two things jump out immediately. First, the memory jump from 16 GB to 32 GB is unusually large for a recommended tier, which hints that the developers expect co-op sessions and larger train builds to lean hard on system memory. Second, the storage figure is identical in both columns, so 25 GB is your real target either way.

## Reading the Frostrail System Requirements Like a Builder

Specs on a store page are just numbers until you translate them into what happens on screen. Here is how each line of the Frostrail system requirements affects your actual play session.

| Component | Minimum tier means | Recommended tier means | Practical impact |

### A note on that processor listing

The recommended Intel chip, the i5-11400T, is a lower-power part that does not clearly outperform the i7-6700K named in the minimum column. This looks like an inconsistency in the store listing rather than a genuine downgrade — and it is common in Early Access pages where specs get updated piecemeal. Community reports on similar Steam listings suggest treating the stronger of the two processors as your realistic target. If you own either an i7-6700K-class chip or a modern six-core Ryzen 5, you are comfortably in range.

Because the recommended tier doubles memory, a RAM bump is often the cheaper path to a smoother experience — especially if you plan to play with friends, where the game has to track multiple players, enemies, and a moving fortress at once.

## How the Frostrail System Requirements May Change in Early Access

Frostrail is heading into Early Access with an expected stay of roughly one year, and the developers have said plainly that the full version will add more biomes, more loot, deeper crafting, train upgrades, and new co-op scenarios. All of that content has a hardware cost.

Historically, Early Access games tend to see specs drift upward as features land, then get pulled back during optimization passes near 1.0. That pattern means two things for you:

1. **Do not buy hardware for a moving target.** If you are one tier short today, wait for launch-week performance reports before spending money.
2. **Do expect the 25 GB storage figure to grow.** Additional biomes and dungeons almost always add install size. Leaving 10–15 GB of headroom on your drive is cheap insurance.

The developers also plan to raise the price at 1.0, so buying early gets you both a lower price and a firsthand look at how the Frostrail system requirements hold up in real sessions.

If you clear the minimum but stutter during blizzards, drop those three first — they are the same effects that make the frozen wasteland look great, which is exactly why they are expensive.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do the Frostrail system requirements include Windows 11?**

The listing names Windows 10 64-bit specifically. In practice, Windows 11 is a 64-bit OS and almost always runs games listed for Windows 10, but the store page does not officially confirm it. If you are on Windows 11, you should be fine — just keep drivers current.

**Can I run Frostrail with 16 GB of RAM?**

Yes, 16 GB is the stated minimum. However, the recommended tier doubles that to 32 GB, so if you plan to host co-op sessions or build out a large train with many crafting stations, 16 GB may produce stutter during heavy moments. Upgrading RAM is usually the cheapest single improvement you can make.

**Is a 25 GB install the final size?**

Not necessarily. That is the current figure for the Frostrail system requirements during Early Access. The full version is planned to add biomes, dungeons, and progression systems, which typically increases install size. Budget extra free space.

**Will Frostrail run on Steam Deck?**

Steam Deck compatibility is currently listed as unknown. Until Valve issues a verification rating or players report their own results, treat handheld play as unconfirmed and check back closer to the Q4 2026 Early Access launch.

## The Bottom Line

The Frostrail system requirements are mid-range by modern standards, but they are not trivial — an RTX 3060 and 16 GB of RAM is a real floor, and the 32 GB recommended memory is the detail most players will miss. Check your GPU, your RAM, and your drive space in that order, keep a little storage headroom for post-launch patches, and hold off on upgrades until Early Access performance reports land. Do that, and the only thing standing between you and the frozen wasteland will be the revenants.
