## What Kind of Game Is Frostrail?

Frostrail is a co-op survival game built around a moving base. You play as one of the Penitent Gardeners, a grim order traveling an empire that an Emperor's pact with the Void turned into a wasteland of ice, revenants, and eldritch horrors. Your job is equal parts mercy and extermination.

### The train is your base, not your vehicle

The armored train — powered by what the page calls the Eden Engine — carries your fuel, storage, crafting stations, and weapon mounts. It is not a level select screen. It is the thing that keeps you alive between expeditions, and it has to be fed. Managing engine power and fuel is a core loop, not a side activity.

Upgrades follow a clear logic: reinforce walls, add weapon stations, improve the engine so it outpaces what hunts you, and bolt on new carriages for crafting and storage. In practice, your progression is measured in how much fortress you can drag behind you.

### The cold is a real enemy

Leaving the tracks is a gamble by design. The store page describes blizzards that blind and slow you, frost that "creeps into your bones," and a simple survival rule: heat is survival, so ration it. Every supply run outside the train trades safety for resources. That tension — risk death for loot or stay warm and go without — is the heart of the design.

Exploration targets include abandoned settlements, wreckage lost to the frost, and deeper dungeon-like locations where better rewards sit behind worse danger.

A few practical tips before you buy in:

- **Check your GPU first.** An RTX 3060-class card as the minimum puts this well above typical indie survival requirements.
- **Budget for RAM.** Going from 16 GB to 32 GB is the single biggest jump between tiers; if you run a browser and Discord alongside games, 32 GB is the safer target.
- **Plan for an SSD.** The listing only specifies 25 GB of space, but load-heavy survival games with large outdoor zones benefit enormously from solid-state storage.
- **Re-check at launch.** Early Access specs shift. Treat this table as a snapshot, not a permanent contract.
