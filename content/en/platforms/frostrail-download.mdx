## What You Are Downloading: The Game in Brief

Understanding the pitch helps you decide whether the download is worth the disk space. The setting is a fallen empire hollowed out by an endless cold, twisted by the Emperor's pact with the Void and now stalked by revenants and eldritch horrors. You play as one of the Penitent Gardeners, riding an armored, steam-powered train through the desolation.

The train is the whole design philosophy in one object. It is shelter, vehicle, and progression system at once — you reinforce its walls, build weapon stations, upgrade the engine, and bolt on new carriages for crafting and storage. Every step away from it is a gamble against blizzards, frostbite, and whatever is moving in the silence of the snow.
