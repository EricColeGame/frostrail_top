## Frostrail PC System Requirements: What Your Rig Needs

The Steam listing publishes both a minimum and a recommended tier, and the gap between them is bigger than it looks at first glance — mostly in memory and graphics.

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| OS | Windows 10 (64-bit) | Windows 10 (64-bit) |
| Processor | Intel i7 6700K or AMD Ryzen 5 5500 | Intel i5 11400T or AMD Ryzen 5 |
| Memory | 16 GB RAM | 32 GB RAM |
| Graphics | GeForce RTX 3060 or AMD Radeon RX 5600 XT | GeForce RTX 4060 or AMD Radeon RX 7600 |
| Storage | 25 GB available space | 25 GB available space |
| Notes | 64-bit processor and OS required | 64-bit processor and OS required |

A few practical observations for anyone planning a Frostrail PC build or upgrade:

- **The GPU jump is the real story.** Moving from an RTX 3060-class card to an RTX 4060-class card suggests the recommended tier targets higher settings or higher frame rates rather than a wildly different renderer.
- **32 GB of RAM is a notable ask.** Doubling memory for the recommended tier is unusual for a game with a 25 GB install footprint, so if you run a browser, Discord, and a capture tool alongside your games, factor that in.
- **The CPU listings look inverted.** The recommended processor reads lower-spec than the minimum on paper. Treat the CPU rows as a rough guide and check community benchmarks once the game is in players' hands rather than assuming the labels are perfectly calibrated.
- **Storage is modest.** At 25 GB, Frostrail is friendlier to a crowded SSD than most modern survival titles.

If your machine sits between the two tiers, you are likely in "playable at medium settings" territory rather than "unplayable." As always with Early Access, expect optimization work to continue after launch.

## Gameplay Pillars: What You Actually Do on PC

Strip away the atmosphere and Frostrail is a loop of train management, dangerous expeditions, and combat. Understanding that loop tells you more about whether the game is for you than any spec sheet.

| Pillar | What It Means in Play |
|---|---|
| Train as home base | Fuel the Eden Engine, manage power, and operate the train to reach new areas |
| Upgrading the train | Reinforce walls, build weapon stations, upgrade the engine, add carriages and crafting stations |
| Expeditions | Leave the train to scavenge ruins, settlements, and wreckage for supplies |
| Cold as an enemy | Blizzards blind and slow you; heat is a resource to ration |
| Dungeon risk | Descend into bunkers and strongholds where rewards and death sit side by side |
| Combat | Fight revenants and eldritch horrors solo or in co-op, crafting weapons and equipment |

The tension the design leans on is simple and effective: every step away from the train is a gamble. Stay warm and safe with nothing, or push into the frost for supplies you may not survive to use. That risk-reward spine is what separates a survival game from a walking simulator, and it is the part most likely to be tuned during Early Access.

A Steam Next Fest demo was part of the promotional run-up, so if you missed it, watching recorded footage is the next best way to judge the combat feel before buying.

## FAQ

**Is Frostrail PC available to buy right now?**
No. The Steam page lists the game as not yet available, with a planned Early Access release in Q4 2026. You can add it to your wishlist to be notified when it goes live.

**What are the Frostrail PC system requirements?**
The minimum tier calls for Windows 10 64-bit, an Intel i7 6700K or AMD Ryzen 5 5500, 16 GB of RAM, a GeForce RTX 3060 or Radeon RX 5600 XT, and 25 GB of storage. The recommended tier raises memory to 32 GB and graphics to an RTX 4060 or RX 7600 class card.

**Does Frostrail support co-op on PC?**
Yes. The game is designed for solo play or banding together with other players to survive combat and exploration in the frozen wasteland. Co-op scenarios are specifically called out as a focus area for the 1.0 version.

**Will the price go up after Early Access?**
The developers have stated they plan to gradually increase the price at the 1.0 release, depending on how much content and how many features are added. Buying during Early Access is therefore likely to be cheaper, provided you are comfortable with an unfinished game.
