What that means in practice: solo is a legitimate way to play, not a compromised mode. With one player you handle fuel, driving, repairs, and defense yourself, which is slow but teaches the resource loop quickly. With two players you can split train and expedition duties. With three or four, you can afford a dedicated guard and a dedicated scavenger at the same time — the setup most co-op screenshots show.

If you are choosing a crew size for your first session, two is the sweet spot for learning and four is the sweet spot for surviving. A full crew of four means more hands but also more mouths to feed on ammunition and crafting resources, which the game deliberately keeps scarce.

## Co-op Roles: Splitting the Work on the Train

The Frostrail playtest guide advice that saves the most runs is simple: assign jobs out loud before you leave the platform. The co-op loop explicitly asks crews to coordinate fuel, power, driving, repair, and train defense, and to split scavenging from train-guarding. In a four-player crew, that maps cleanly onto four informal roles.

| Role | Core duty | Best crew size | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|---|
| Driver | Keep the train moving; manage speed and route choices | Any | Leaving the cab empty when a defense starts |
| Fuel and power hand | Track reserves; ration for the next stretch | 2+ | Burning the reserve before a known encounter |
| Scavenger | Push out to buildings like the frozen chapel for supplies | 2+ | Roaming too far with no return plan |
| Guard | Hold the carriage and cover the scavengers' return | 3–4 | Assuming engine noise gives a fixed safe distance |

A few coordination rules that consistently show up in player experience:

- **Share ammunition and crafting resources early.** Hoarding in a four-player crew starves the person actually holding the line.
- **Pick exits as a group.** Multiple level exits exist, and a crew that splits its decision ends up split on the map.
- **Rotate the boring job.** Whoever drives first should not drive all night; fatigue on one role is how repairs get missed.
- **Call your scavenging runs.** A short "going out, back in ninety seconds" keeps the guard from wandering off the carriage.

For solo players, the same roles collapse into a rotation: drive, stop, scavenge close to the train, return, defend. Shorter trips and closer buildings are the whole strategy.

## FAQ: Frostrail Playtest Guide Quick Answers

**How many players can join a Frostrail playtest crew?**
One to four. The official FAQ states a crew range of 1–4, and the store listing presents the game as single-player plus online co-op. No test so far has announced a different crew-size rule.

**How did people get into the April playtest?**
Through Steam's Friend Invite feature. An accepted player could invite one Steam friend, who could then invite another. This was a test-access mechanic and not a commitment to how the final game will handle invitations.

**Is this Frostrail playtest guide based on the released game?**
No. All publicly shown footage and screenshots come from a pre-release build, so interfaces and item values can still change before Early Access.

**Does Frostrail support split-screen or cross-play?**
Neither has been announced. Local co-op, split-screen, cross-play, and cross-save are all currently listed as undetailed, so plan for online co-op on Steam with friends who own the game.
